Port Pass Pro - Visitor Request Guide

This document provides step-by-step instructions for submitting and tracking a visitor request using the internal portal at http://localhost:5173/visitor-request.

Submitting a New Request

By default, you will land on the New Request tab, which consists of a 3-step process:

Step 1: Requester (Your Information)

This section is for the details of the person submitting the request (the internal employee).

  • Your Name: Enter your full name (Required).
  • Email Address: Enter your company email address (Required). You will need this later to track the request.
  • Phone Number: Enter your contact number (Optional).

Click Next Step to proceed.

Step 2: Visitor Details

This section is for the details of the guest who will be visiting the port.

  • Visitor Name: Enter the guest's full name (Required).
  • NIC / Passport: Enter their national ID card or passport number. They will need to present this at the gate (Required).
  • Visitor Phone: The guest's contact number (Optional).
  • Company / Organization: The company the guest is representing (Optional).

Click Next Step to proceed.

Step 3: Visit Info

This section specifies the logistics and routing of the visit.

  • Purpose of Visit: Briefly describe why they are visiting (e.g., "Maintenance", "Sales Meeting") (Required).
  • Department to Visit: Select the internal department the visitor is going to from the dropdown menu (Required). This request will automatically route to that department's manager for approval.
  • Date & Time: Select the expected Entry and Exit dates/times. Note: You can only schedule visits up to 30 days in advance (Required).
  • Zones to Access: Select one or more areas the visitor needs access to (e.g., Zone 1 Office, Zone 2 Warehouse) (Required).
  • Vehicle Number: If the visitor is bringing a car, enter the license plate number (Optional).

Click Submit Request to finalize.

Tracking Your Request

Once submitted, you will be given a Request ID. To check if the pass has been approved:

  1. Go to the Track Request tab at the top of the page.
  2. Enter your Request ID.
  3. Enter the Email Address you used in Step 1.
  4. Click Track Status.

The system will show if the request is Pending Department Approval, Awaiting Security Verification, Issued, or Rejected.

Note on Group Passes: The third tab, "Group Pass Issue", is restricted. It can only be accessed by authorized Department Managers or Administrators who have signed into the system.
